# Market research

This directory contains non-normative market and competitive research for the People Management Platform.

Research artifacts may inform product, procurement, UX, and architecture decisions, but they do not override [`docs/project-requirements.md`](../project-requirements.md). Any recommendation adopted by the team must be captured separately as an approved decision or architecture record.

## Documents

- [People Management, Talent, and Engineering Resourcing Market Landscape, 2026](people-management-market-landscape-2026.md) — competitive landscape, capability benchmark, evidence ledger, and target-platform assessment. Research cutoff: 20 August 2026.

## Maintenance

- Keep the research cutoff and review status in each document's metadata current.
- Prefer durable primary-source links for vendor and technical claims.
- Revalidate time-sensitive claims before using them in procurement or architecture decisions.
- Do not copy research conclusions into normative requirements without explicit product-owner approval.
